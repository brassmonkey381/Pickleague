Delete your Pickleague account

Pickleague (iOS, Android and pickleague.club). Last updated 10 November 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Sign in on your phone, or at pickleague.club.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

If you can't sign in or no longer have the app, email us from the address you signed up with:

Email a deletion request

To: support@pickleague.club, subject "Delete my Pickleague account". If you write from a different address, tell us the email on the account; we will confirm with that address before deleting anything. We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in: email address, password and sessions.
  • Your profile: name, username, avatar, tagline, paddle and home court.
  • Your court check-ins, play sessions, routines and court settings.
  • Your pickle history, shop items and My Place.
  • Your push notification tokens and notifications.

What is kept

  • Matches you played with other people stay, so their ratings and standings remain correct. Your name on them is replaced with "[deleted account]".
  • Copies in encrypted backups expire on their own shortly after deletion.
