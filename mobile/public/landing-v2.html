Standings and ratings

Every game counts toward something.

Enter the score on any phone or laptop. Once the other side confirms it, the standings and everyone's PLUPR skill rating update on their own. Beat a stronger team and you move up more. Rounds lock in, and your season finish is your median round rank.

Every round starts back at the league's baseline rating (3.50 here). Sample league, names, numbers and pot.
